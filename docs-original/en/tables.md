# Building Tables

## Creating a Cross-Tabulation

1.  Go to the `Tables` tab
2.  Drag a question into the `Rows` field
3.  Drag a question into the `Columns` field
4.  Click the `Build Table` button

## Table Settings

### Statistics that can be enabled:
- ✅ Column percentages
- ✅ Row percentages
- ✅ Total percentage
- ✅ Mean
- ✅ Median
- ✅ Standard deviation
- ✅ Standard error of mean
- ✅ Minimum / Maximum
- ✅ Number of respondents (N)

### Additional settings:
- 🔹 Hide zero cells
- 🔹 Row/column sorting
- 🔹 Cell merging
- 🔹 Significance testing

## TopBox and BottomBox

TopBoxes allow you to combine top alternatives of a question into one group (e.g., ratings 9+10).

### Adding a TopBox:
1.  Select a question
2.  Right-click → `Add TopBox`
3.  Specify which alternatives to combine
